RALLY 01 · 2025WON ✓

Streaming Platform Engagement & Churn Simulator

How do people drift from "just browsing" to bingeing, subscribing, or leaving? I modelled Netflix-style user behaviour as a 6-state Markov chain (Visitor → Binge → Subscriber → Churn), found its steady state with power iteration and checked it was ergodic.

Then I ran a Monte Carlo simulation (1,000 users × 50 steps, with optional Poisson arrivals) that landed on a simulated 31% free-to-paid conversion rate, and put it all in a 5-page Streamlit dashboard with live sensitivity analysis, transition heatmaps and funnel metrics.

PythonNumPyPandasStreamlitPlotlyMarkov chains

RALLY 02 · 2025WON ✓

Bitcoin Fear & Greed: Sentiment vs. Profitability

Does "buy fear, sell greed" actually work? I tested it on 200,000+ real trades from 32 traders with two regression models: trade PnL against the Fear & Greed index and trade side, and next-day BTC returns against the index.

The verdict: statistically significant evidence that fear favours buyers at the trade level, but only a weak signal at the daily price level. Visualised across a 5-page Streamlit dashboard.

PythonPandasscikit-learnLinear regressionStreamlit
